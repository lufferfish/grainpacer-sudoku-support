Privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026

No account is required, and the game works offline. We do not collect, upload, sell or share personal data through this app. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Puzzles in progress, notes, elapsed time and completion records are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. System backups may include this data depending on your Apple backup settings. The app does not provide cross-device sync. Deleting the app normally removes local saves; manage system backups through Apple’s settings.

If you contact the developer, the email and attachments you provide are used to handle your support request. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information. Apple’s store, system diagnostics and backup services follow Apple’s own privacy policies.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日

本应用无需账号，可离线游玩。我们不通过本应用收集、上传、出售或共享个人数据；不接入广告、分析、追踪或第三方 SDK。

棋局、笔记、用时和完成记录保存在设备的应用私有空间。系统备份可能包含这些数据，取决于你的 Apple 备份设置；本应用不提供跨设备同步。删除应用通常会删除本机存档，系统备份的管理请使用 Apple 提供的设置。

如你主动联系开发者，你提供的邮件和附件仅用于处理支持请求，请勿附带不必要的个人信息。Apple 的商店、系统诊断或备份服务遵循 Apple 自己的隐私政策。
