Support

Reveal colorful pixel pictures with 300 offline nonogram puzzles for iPhone and iPad. Follow the row and column numbers, tap or drag to fill squares, and mark crosses to remember blank squares. Undo and redo are available. Each game has up to 3 hints; replaying a completed level resets the hint allowance.

Progress and remaining hints save automatically on your device. To resume, choose Continue or select a level. There is no cross-device sync. Deleting the app normally removes local progress.

For help, email lufferfish@gmail.com. Include the app version, device model and a description of the issue. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

支持

谷步数织包含 300 个离线关卡，支持 iPhone 和 iPad。根据行列数字点击或拖动填格，用叉号标记空格，支持撤销和重做。每局最多 3 次提示，通关后重新游玩恢复次数。进度和剩余次数自动保存在设备，不提供跨设备同步。删除应用通常会删除本机进度。如需帮助，请联系上方邮箱，并提供版本、机型及问题描述。

Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026.

No account is required. The game works offline and does not collect, upload, sell or share personal data. It contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Puzzle progress, completion records and remaining hint counts are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Apple system backups may include these files, depending on your settings. The app does not provide cross-device sync. Apple’s store, diagnostics and backup services are governed by Apple’s privacy policies.

If you contact support, the email and attachments you choose to send are used to respond to your request.

隐私政策
