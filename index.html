Help & support

A quiet, offline Sudoku game for iPhone and iPad. Explore 300 levels across Easy, Medium and Hard, or start a random practice puzzle.

How to play

Use 1–9 once in each row, column and 3×3 box. Select an empty cell, then a number. Use Notes for candidates, Erase to clear an entry and Undo to take a move back.

Hints

Step-by-step hints let you observe, read the reasoning and reveal the conclusion. They do not fill cells automatically. The timer pauses while you read.

Saved games

Each level saves separately. Starting new random practice replaces the previous practice game. Saves stay on your device; the app does not sync across devices. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot, because this may remove your progress.

帮助与支持

为 iPhone 和 iPad 设计的离线数独。简单、中等、困难各 100 关，也可以开始随机练习。

怎么玩

每行、每列、每个九宫格中，1–9 各出现一次。先选空格，再点数字。使用笔记记录候选数，擦除清空输入，撤销退回上一步。

逐步提示

先观察，再查看推理，最后揭示结论。提示不会自动填数，阅读时计时暂停。

存档

每关独立保存。新的随机练习会替换上一局练习。进度存储在设备本地，应用不提供跨设备同步。排查问题时请勿删除应用，以免丢失进度。
